Welcome

dicta2stream is a minimalist voice streaming platform for your spoken audio anonymously under a nickname in a loop. (Opus | Mono | 48 kHz | 60 kbps)

What you can do here:

  • 🎧 Listen to public voice streams from others, instantly
  • 🎙️ Upload your own audio and share your voice with the world
  • 🕵️ No sign-up required for listening
  • 🔒 Optional registration for uploading and managing your own stream
